Batting average = cars EW actually bought ÷ cars the dealer submitted, matched VIN by VIN. A dash means no cars have been tagged to that dealer yet — deliberately not shown as 0%, which would read as "they sent cars and we bought none." Tagging starts on the bid page; cars submitted before 2026-07-31 carry no dealer, so the measured set grows from here.
EW profit per row is what EW made on that relationship in both directions — the profit on cars we sold them, plus the profit on cars we bought from them and resold. It is profit, not revenue.
The rows do not add up to the headline, and should not. A car bought from one dealer and sold to another credits that single deal's profit to both relationships — correct for judging each dealer, wrong as a total. The headline is computed once from the deals ledger ($35.4M), not by summing the column ($71.1M).
Dealers are suppliers with a licence or tax cert on file. The All rows filter also shows private sellers and marketplaces that appear in the same table.
Any column sorts — click a heading, click again to flip. Default is the highest batting average first.
